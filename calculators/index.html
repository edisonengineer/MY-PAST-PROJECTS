Sheet metal

Bend allowance & flat length

Work out how long to cut a flat blank so it bends to the right size. Enter the outside lengths of the two flanges, the material thickness, the inside bend radius and the K-factor.

How it's calculated
  • Bend allowance BA = angle (radians) × (R + K × T)
  • Outside setback OSSB = tan(angle ÷ 2) × (R + T)
  • Bend deduction BD = 2 × OSSB − BA
  • Flat length = A + B − BD

The K-factor is where the neutral axis sits through the thickness. It depends on the material, tooling and bending method, and is usually 0.3 to 0.5. Ask your fabricator for theirs.

Print design

Print size & DPI

See how many pixels a design needs to print sharply, and check whether a photo or logo you already have is big enough.

What DPI should I use?
  • 300 DPI: flyers, posters, certificates and anything seen up close.
  • 150 DPI: large banners and roll-ups viewed from a few metres.
  • Bleed: an extra 3 mm around the edge so nothing white shows after trimming.
