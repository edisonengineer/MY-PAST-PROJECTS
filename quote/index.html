          How the price is worked out
          • Flat length = sum of the outside flange lengths − a bend deduction for each bend. The bend deduction uses your thickness, inside radius and K-factor (the same method as the bend allowance calculator).
          • Parts per sheet: how many blanks fit in rows on the sheet, with 10 mm round the edge and 5 mm between parts, trying both ways round.
          • Material: whole sheets used × sheet price, or the weight of the parts × price per kg + scrap allowance.
          • Cutting: the blank's outline plus the holes' cut length, × your price per metre, plus a charge per pierce.
          • Price to the customer = (material + cutting + bending + finish + labour + set-up) × (1 + markup), then delivery and VAT if chosen.

          The example prices are only a starting point. Put in what you pay and charge, and they are remembered on this device.
