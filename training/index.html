Do I need my own software?

Yes, you will need the software installed on your laptop. Autodesk and SolidWorks offer free or low-cost licences for students and educators, and I will help you choose and set up the right option before the first lesson.

What kind of computer do I need?

A laptop that meets the software's minimum requirements and a mouse with a scroll wheel. Tell me your laptop model when you reserve a seat and I will check it for you.

I have never used CAD before. Can I join?

Absolutely. Every course starts from the basics and builds up step by step with practical, project-based exercises.

How are the lessons scheduled?

We agree on the days and times together. Evening and weekend sessions are available, online or in person in Nairobi.

How do I pay?

Once we agree on the schedule, you can pay by M-Pesa. Prices start from KES 4,500 per trainee, depending on the course length and format.
