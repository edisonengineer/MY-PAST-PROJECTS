What services do you offer?

Two kinds of work through Nexus CAD Studio. Engineering design: 3D CAD modelling, 2D manufacturing drawings, FEA/CFD simulation, sheet metal and fabrication design, product renders and CAD training. Graphic design: posters, branding, certificates, social media graphics, print layouts and motion graphics.

Which software do you work with?

For engineering: SolidWorks (including CSWA exam preparation), Autodesk Inventor, AutoCAD, Fusion 360, Revit and Abaqus. For graphics: Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign and Premiere Pro. If you need files in a specific program, just tell me in the project brief.

How do I start a project?

Fill in the project brief above. It takes about two minutes and covers everything I need to understand your project. You can also message me directly on WhatsApp.

How much will my project cost?

Pricing depends on complexity, the files you need and how soon you need them. Once I have your brief, I'll send a clear quote before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A single poster is much quicker than a full assembly with simulation. We agree on a timeline together when I send your quote, and I'll keep you updated along the way.

Can you work from my sketches, photos or existing files?

Yes. Hand sketches with dimensions, photos, physical samples or existing CAD files are all great starting points. The more detail you share, the faster and more accurate the result.

What files will I receive?

Whatever suits your project. For engineering: native CAD files, STEP/IGES, STL for 3D printing, DWG/DXF and PDF drawings. For graphics: print-ready PDFs and high-resolution PNG/JPG, with source files available on request.

Do you include revisions?

Yes. Revisions are agreed as part of your quote, so we can refine the design until it's right for you.

Do you work with clients outside Nairobi?

Yes. I work with clients remotely: briefs, files and reviews are shared online, and training can be delivered online or in person in Nairobi.
