How it works
  • Theory questions are multiple choice: drawings, sketching, features, assemblies, mass and units.
  • Modelling questions give a dimensioned drawing (third-angle projection) and the material. Model the part in SolidWorks, open Mass Properties and type the mass in grams. Answers within 1% count as right.
  • Follow-up questions ask you to change one size of the part you just made, like the real exam.
  • CSWP-style parts come in 3 stages: build the part, then change sizes (and sometimes the material) twice, entering the mass each time. The last question asks for the centre of mass (X, Y, Z) from the origin marked O. Answers within 1% count, like the real exam.
  • The exam generator picks the parts, sizes, materials and theory numbers at random, so every exam is new. Choose timed or untimed.
  • Your test is saved as you go, so a dropped connection or a closed tab doesn't lose it.
